Vurb vs built-in dictation and other dictation apps

How Vurb differs from the dictation built into your operating system, and how its prices compare with other dictation apps.

Vurb and built-in dictation

macOS and Windows have dictation built in. If you use it now and then and it does what you need, you may not need Vurb. Features differ by version, so check what yours does.

Here's what Vurb does, so you can compare:

Hold-to-talk with one key
Hold a key, talk, let go, in any app. Double-tap for hands-free, Esc to cancel.
Local Whisper models
OpenAI Whisper via whisper.cpp, running on your computer. You choose the model: Fastest, Fast, Balanced or Best.
Custom vocabulary
Your names and terms, spelled your way.
History and stats
Search everything you've dictated; see words per minute, streaks and time saved.
Optional AI cleanup
Punctuation, filler removal and self-corrections, with your own Claude key.
Same app everywhere
macOS, Windows and Linux, with the same settings and behaviour.
