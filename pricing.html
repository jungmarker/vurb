Pricing questions

Is the free plan actually free?

Yes. 2,000 words a week, all languages, hold-to-talk and hands-free, history and stats. No card and no account.

What counts as a word?

Words Vurb types for you. The weekly count is worked out on your computer from your local history and resets each week.

Does Pro include AI cleanup?

Not yet. Built-in cleanup without your own key is coming. Today every plan can use cleanup with your own Claude API key.

How does Growth billing work?

Per seat, with a 3-seat minimum. Billed monthly or yearly on one invoice. Add or remove seats anytime.

What's the refund policy?

Pro and Growth have a 30-day money-back guarantee. Ask within 30 days of buying and we'll refund you in full.

Who handles payment?

Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record. They handle the card details and sales tax.
