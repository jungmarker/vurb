Hold a key. Talk. Let go.

Vurb types what you said wherever your cursor is — an email, a chat window, a code editor, a form. Transcription runs on your own computer, so your audio never leaves it, and it works offline.

Download for free See pricing

The key is fn on a Mac and Right Control on Windows and Linux. You can change it.

How it works

  1. 1

    Hold the key

    Click into any text field first. Then hold your dictation key. A small pill appears at the bottom of the screen while it listens.

  2. 2

    Talk

    Say it the way you'd say it out loud. It detects the language on its own, so switching between English and Spanish is fine.

  3. 3

    Let go

    The text is typed where your cursor is. Press Esc before letting go to throw it away.

For longer stretches, double-tap the key. It keeps listening until you tap it again.

Why we built it

We talk faster than we type, and we wanted that to work in every app on the computer, not just inside one product. We also didn't want our voice sent to a server just to get a sentence into a chat window. OpenAI's Whisper model runs fast enough on a laptop now, so we put it behind a single key.

That's most of the app. It transcribes 12 seconds of speech in about 0.3 seconds on an M-series Mac with the best model. The free plan is meant to be usable every day, and the paid plans are priced so a whole team can afford them.

— The Vurb team

Questions

Does my audio leave my computer?

No. Audio is transcribed on your computer and stays there. If you turn on AI cleanup, the transcribed text is sent for cleanup. Never the audio. Cleanup is off until you turn it on.

Which operating systems does it run on?

macOS, Windows and Linux. Downloads are on GitHub Releases. It's fastest on Apple Silicon Macs.

Is the free plan actually free?

Yes. 2,000 words a week, all languages, hold-to-talk and hands-free, history and stats. No card and no account. You can use AI cleanup on Free with your own Claude API key. Pro removes the word limit.

Does Pro include AI cleanup?

Not yet. Built-in cleanup that doesn't need your own API key is coming. Today every plan can use cleanup with your own Claude API key.

Do you have team plans?

Growth gives every seat everything in Pro, on one invoice, with seats you can add or remove anytime (3-seat minimum). Enterprise adds volume discounts, invoicing and POs, annual contracts, security review and DPA support, and a dedicated success manager with an SLA. Email sales.

Will it pass our security review?

Transcription is 100% on-device on every plan, so employee audio never leaves their computers. AI cleanup sends text only and is off by default. On Enterprise we'll go through your security review and help with a DPA.

What's the refund policy?

Pro and Growth have a 30-day money-back guarantee. If it isn't for you, ask within 30 days of buying and we'll refund you in full.

Will AI cleanup change what I said?

It adds punctuation and removes filler like "um" and "uh". It doesn't rewrite your sentences into something else, and it never translates.
