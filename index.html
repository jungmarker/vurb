FAQ

Good questions

Does my audio leave my computer?

No. Your audio is transcribed locally and never leaves your computer. If you turn on optional AI cleanup, only the transcribed text is sent for cleanup — never the audio. Cleanup is off unless you enable it.

Which operating systems are supported?

macOS, Windows, and Linux. Downloads are on GitHub Releases. Transcription is fastest on Apple Silicon Macs.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 2,000 words per week, all languages, hold-to-talk and hands-free, and history & stats — no card, no account. You can also use AI cleanup on Free with your own Claude API key. Pro removes the word limit.

Does Pro include AI cleanup?

Not yet. Built-in AI cleanup that doesn't need your own API key is coming soon. Today, every plan can use AI cleanup by adding your own Claude API key.

Do you have plans for teams?

Yes. Growth gives every seat everything in Pro, with one invoice, centralized billing, and seats you can add or remove anytime (3-seat minimum). Enterprise adds volume discounts, invoicing/PO and annual contracts, security review and DPA support, and a dedicated success manager with an SLA. Talk to sales.

Is it a fit for compliance-sensitive teams?

Transcription runs 100% on-device on every plan, so employee audio never leaves their computers. Optional AI cleanup sends text only, and it's off unless turned on. Enterprise customers get security review and DPA support.

What's your refund policy?

Pro and Growth have a 30-day money-back guarantee. If Vurb.me isn't for you, ask within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.

Does AI cleanup change what I said?

It adds punctuation and removes filler words like "um" and "uh". It never translates your words into another language.
