0.1.0

Beta · first public release

The first public version of Vurb, for macOS, Windows and Linux.

  • Hold-to-talk dictation into any app, with double-tap hands-free and Esc to cancel
  • Choose your dictation key (fn on Mac and Right Control on Windows/Linux by default)
  • Local transcription with OpenAI Whisper via whisper.cpp; four model choices from Fastest to Best
  • 90+ languages with automatic detection
  • Custom vocabulary
  • Optional AI cleanup with your own Claude API key
  • History with search, and stats: words per minute, streaks, time saved, badges
  • Start/stop sounds with volume control, and the waveform pill while recording
  • Launch at login
  • Free plan (2,000 words a week) and Pro and Growth licenses
