Use cases / Accessibility

Type less

If typing is painful, tiring or slow for you — because of RSI, limited hand mobility, dyslexia or anything else — Vurb lets you write by talking in any app. It's a dictation tool, not a medical device, and it doesn't control the rest of your computer by voice.
