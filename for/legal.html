Use cases / Lawyers

Dictation for lawyers that keeps the audio on your machine

Vurb transcribes on your own computer, so recordings of what you say about a matter aren't sent to a transcription service. Whether that's enough for your obligations is for you and your firm to decide.

Useful features

On-device transcription

Audio is transcribed locally and discarded. It isn't sent to us or anyone else.

Local history

Transcripts and history are stored on your computer only. You can clear them.

AI cleanup stays off

Cleanup is off by default. If you enable it, the text goes to Anthropic under your own key, so leave it off for sensitive work if your policies require.
