Use cases / Founders & managers

Write the update you keep putting off

Most of a manager's job is writing things down: updates, feedback, decisions, answers. Talking makes it quicker to get them out.

Workflows

Examples of what you'd say and what ends up in the text field.

Weekly updates

Talk through the week in the doc or channel where it goes.

Said

“This week we shipped the new onboarding flow, hired our second support person, and pushed the pricing change to next month”

Typed

This week we shipped the new onboarding flow, hired our second support person, and pushed the pricing change to next month.

Feedback

Specific feedback is long. Say it instead of shortening it.

Said

“The demo was strong. Next time I'd start with the customer problem before the product, it took a while to see why it mattered”

Typed

The demo was strong. Next time I'd start with the customer problem before the product; it took a while to see why it mattered.

Decisions

Write down what was decided and why, right after the meeting.

Said

“We're going with the annual plan only for enterprise, because procurement needs a fixed contract”

Typed

We're going with the annual plan only for enterprise, because procurement needs a fixed contract.
