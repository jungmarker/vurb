Everything Vurb does today

One key, any app, on your own computer. Here's the full list, with the things we haven't shipped yet marked as such.

Dictation

Hold to talk
Hold your key, speak, let go. The text is typed at your cursor.
Hands-free
Double-tap the key to keep listening without holding it. Tap once more to stop.
Cancel
Press Esc while recording and nothing is typed.
Works in any app
It types into whatever has focus. If pasting isn't allowed yet, the text is copied so you can paste it yourself.
Choose your key
fn by default on a Mac, Right Control on Windows and Linux. You can pick Option/Alt, Command/Windows, Control or Right Shift instead.

Languages and accuracy

90+ languages
Whisper detects the language automatically. Mixing English and Spanish in the same day, or the same sentence, works.
Speech models
Pick the trade-off: Fastest (English only, 148 MB), Fast (148 MB), Balanced (190 MB) or Best (574 MB). Apple Silicon Macs default to Best; other machines default to Balanced.
Speed
About 0.3s to transcribe a 12-second clip on an M-series Mac with the Best model.
Custom vocabulary
Add names, product terms and jargon so they come out spelled right.
Offline
Transcription runs on your computer. Once a model is downloaded, no internet connection is needed to dictate.

AI cleanup (optional)

What it does
Fixes punctuation, removes filler words like “um” and “uh”, and keeps only the correction when you change your mind mid-sentence (“no wait, I mean Thursday”).
What it doesn't do
It never translates, and it doesn't rewrite what you said into something else.
How it runs
Uses Claude with your own Anthropic API key, on any plan. Off by default. Only the text is sent, never audio.
Built-in cleanup coming soon
Cleanup on Pro without needing your own key.

History and stats

History
Every dictation is saved on your computer, with search.
Stats

Comfort

Sounds

Launch at login
