How far 2,000 words goes

People typically speak at about 130–150 words per minute, so 2,000 words is roughly 15 minutes of talking a week. That covers a lot of emails, chat replies and notes. It's meant to be usable every day, not a demo.

It won't cover drafting long documents or dictating most of your writing. That's what Pro is for.

Getting started for free

Download Vurb for Mac, Windows or Linux from the download page and open it. There's nothing to sign up for. The app downloads a speech model the first time (148–574 MB depending on which one), and after that transcription runs on your computer, even offline.

Click into any text field, hold your dictation key — fn on a Mac, Right Control on Windows and Linux — and talk. Let go and the text is typed. Your weekly word count is shown in the app, so you'll always know where you stand before you reach the limit.

Questions about the free plan

Is Vurb's free plan really free?

Yes. No card and no account. You get 2,000 words a week with local transcription, all languages, hold-to-talk and hands-free, and history and stats.

What happens when I reach 2,000 words?

The weekly limit resets each week. If you regularly need more, Pro removes the limit.

Can I use AI cleanup on the free plan?

Yes, with your own Anthropic (Claude) API key. Built-in cleanup without your own key is coming to Pro.

Is there a refund if I upgrade and change my mind?

Pro and Growth have a 30-day money-back guarantee.
